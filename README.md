## 🌍 Overview

Project Ares Genesis is a conceptual aerospace engineering project that explores how humanity could build the first permanent and self-sustaining city on Mars.

The project focuses on autonomous robotics, artificial intelligence, renewable energy, local resource utilization, and sustainable life-support systems to prepare Mars for human settlement before astronauts arrive.

---

# 🎯 Mission

Build a Mars city capable of producing:

- 💧 Water
- 🌬 Oxygen
- ⚡ Energy
- ⛽ Rocket Fuel
- 🌱 Food

using local Martian resources.

---

# 🛰 Mission Philosophy

Instead of sending astronauts first,

Project Ares Genesis proposes sending autonomous robots that can:

- Explore Mars
- Build infrastructure
- Mine resources
- Produce energy
- Construct habitats
- Prepare life-support systems

Only after the city is fully operational will astronauts arrive.

## Phase 4 — Water Production

Robots mine underground ice.

Water is:

- Purified
- Stored
- Recycled

Used for:

- Drinking
- Farming
- Fuel Production
- Oxygen Production

---

## Phase 5 — Oxygen Production

Mars atmosphere contains approximately 95% Carbon Dioxide (CO₂).

The oxygen plant:

- Collects CO₂
- Converts CO₂ into Oxygen
- Stores Oxygen
- Supplies habitats

---

## Phase 6 — Rocket Fuel Production

Rocket fuel is produced on Mars.

Resources:

- CO₂ from atmosphere
- Hydrogen from water

Products:

- Methane (CH₄)
- Liquid Oxygen (LOX)

Applications:

- Return missions
- Surface transportation
- Backup power

---

- Climate control

Example crops:

- Potatoes
- Wheat
- Beans
- Lettuce
- Tomatoes
- Algae

---

## Phase 8 — Human Arrival

# 🤖 Robotics

Project Ares Genesis includes:

- Construction Robots
- Mining Robots
- Maintenance Robots
- Farming Robots
- Inspection Drones

Robots operate autonomously with AI assistance.

---

# 🧠 AI & Human Supervision

Human engineers supervise operations from Earth by sending high-level commands.

Because communication between Earth and Mars can take several minutes, robots must make many local decisions on their own using onboard AI rather than relying on continuous remote control.
